Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MEALCARTA

How do I become a host on MEALCARTA

Becoming a host is easy! Visit our "Become a Host" page and fill out the application form. Our team will review your submission and get back to you within 3-5 business days. Once approved, you can start creating experiences and building your community.

What types of experiences can I host?

MEALCARTA supports a wide range of experiences including culinary events, workshops, outdoor adventures, art classes, and social gatherings. As long as your experience brings people together in a meaningful way and follows our community guidelines, it's welcome on our platform.

How are payments processed for events?

We handle all payments securely through our platform. Attendees pay when they book an experience, and hosts receive their earnings (minus our service fee) 24 hours after the event concludes. We support all major credit cards and digital wallets.

What safety measures does MEALCARTA have in place?

Safety is our top priority. All hosts go through a verification process, and we have a review system for both hosts and attendees. We also provide safety guidelines for different types of experiences and offer 24/7 support for any concerns that may arise.

How do I find experiences near me?

Simply visit our "Explore Experiences" page and enter your location. You can filter by date, category, price, and more. Our algorithm also recommends experiences based on your interests and past attendance.

Can I host virtual experiences on MEALCARTA

Absolutely! We support both in-person and virtual experiences. For virtual events, we provide integration with popular video platforms and tools to help you create engaging online gatherings.
